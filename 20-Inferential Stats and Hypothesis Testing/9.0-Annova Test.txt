Analysis of variance(ANNOVA)

Def: ANNOA is a statistical method used to compare the means of 2 or more groups.

ANNOVA:
Factors
Levels

EG:
Medicine    ~[Factor]
       (dosage)  5mg 10mg 15mg   ~[levels]

Mode of payment    ~[Factor]
       G-pay  phone-pe  IMPS   NEFT   ~[levels]

Assumptions in ANNOVA:

Normality of sampling distribution of mean: The distribution of sample mean is normally distributed (follow gaussian distribution).
Absence of Outliers: Outlying score need to be removed from the dataset.
Homogeneity of Variance: Population variance in different levels of each independent levels of each independent variables are equal.
Samples are independent and randomly selected.

Types of ANNOVA: [3 types]

One way ANNOVA: One factor with at least 2 levels. these levels are independent.
eg. Doctor wants to test a new medication to decrease headache. They split the participants in 3 conditions [10mg, 20mg, 30mg]. Doctor ask the participants to rate the headache (1-10).

Medication --> Factor
10mg   20mg    30mg
  5           7           2
  3           4           6
  _            _           _ 
  _            _           _

2. Repeated Measures of ANNOVA: One factor with at least 2 levels, levels are dependent

             *Running --> Factor*

Levels - Day1   Day2   Day3
                8         5          4
                7         4          9
                _          _          _

3. Factorial ANNOVA: Two or more factors (each of which with at least) 2 levels can be independent and dependent.

             *Running --> Factor*

Levels - Day1   Day2   Day3
                8         5          4
     Male   9         4          3
(Factor<-Gender)
                2         4          6
  Female   7         8          3
(Factor<-Gender)
